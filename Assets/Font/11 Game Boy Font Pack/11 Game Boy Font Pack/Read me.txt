Ahoy, thanks for downloading this asset pack. 

You'll find each font has been organised into four folders.
Two for mono and two for variable (dark and light versions). 

All ready to be dropped into GB Studio!

You'll also find two bulk images containing every font on the 
same sheet, along with two screenshots examples. 

The examples use the variable width versions, as this is the most
recommended usage of the fonts. The monospace versions are included
for those inclined for that mega retro feel. 

Hope you enjoy the fonts.
